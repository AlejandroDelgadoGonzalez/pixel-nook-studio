# Story and renderer contract

- The reader's Screen format setting may center and uniformly scale a locked 9:16 or 16:9 player inside the device viewport. Renderers choose orientation and camera dimensions from `scene.scale.width/height`, never the browser window. Use `scene.readingHeight` for the reading reserve or `platform/rendering/dialogue-bounds.js` to convert the DOM caption boundary into logical canvas coordinates. Format changes preserve the current beat, clock, model, pause and reading mode; independent video export retains its own dimensions and safe margins.

- A manifest may declare fileAudio, a root-relative local JSON audio sheet. It owns local MP3 URLs, chapter/scene scope, gain settings and cue offsets/windows keyed by stable step IDs. Decoded real files may be mixed, faded and looped; no new procedural synthesis is allowed. The player and independent exporter read the same sheet without modifying story state or timing. Unavailable files remain silent, with no synthetic fallback or provider/API calls. Generation occurs once through the connected ElevenLabs plugin outside runtime/QA and is recorded in assets/audio/. Existing approved legacy-story soundtracks remain outside the Alice pilot migration.

- A text beat may explicitly declare `readingDurationMs` and a `during` timeline (`initial`, `effects`, `duration`, optional `delayMs`, `paths`, `motion`, `linear`). This opt-in keeps visible action and reading on one engine clock, rather than adding a silent synchronization wait. Existing text timing is unchanged. Duration must allow the full line to reveal and be read. Text reconstruction applies earlier timelines' initial and final states; advancing commits the final state, pausing freezes motion, and independent export runs the same timeline. Constant-speed paths retain their two-mode profile and exact integral; aquatic linear movement is distinct from ground locomotion. All affected stories require regression after engine changes.

- `captionPresentation: "ready-complete"` presents a complete caption at full opacity only when the paragraph is fully revealed on the reading clock and its optional `captionStartMs` has elapsed. Until then the whole card is invisible and inaccessible, with its measured layout reserve preserved. Reader and independent export share this readiness predicate. C3 uses this presentation at Alejandro's request: its opening caption starts after the 6000 ms ascent/reframe and retains 3000 ms for reading afterward. Other beats retain their durations; stories without this opt-in keep their approved progressive presentation. Do not show an empty/loading card or fade a ready-complete card into view.

- Revision2026-11-12: ready-complete text is complete from beat entry; ordinary dialogue is immediately visible and advances in one click. Deferred captions remain hidden until both captionStartMs and visualMotionRemainingMs are satisfied. Static silent conversation bridges are forbidden; explicitly authorized revisions may end directly on their final text via endAtLastBeat, with saved aliases for removed beats. C3 camera pans in Y only at fixed conversation zoom during hidden-card physical actions.
